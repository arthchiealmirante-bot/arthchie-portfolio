02 / ABOUT

Built from the
workflow outward.

My work sits at the intersection of investigation, crime analytics, operational monitoring, and information systems.

I focus on turning repetitive, fragmented, or difficult-to-monitor processes into tools that are easier to use and easier to act on. That means understanding the actual workflow first—what data comes in, who needs to validate it, what decisions follow, and what information must remain visible.

The result is not software for software's sake. It is practical digital support for reporting, data classification, compliance monitoring, case tracking, and operational analysis.
